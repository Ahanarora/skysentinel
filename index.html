How It Works

How Sky Sentinel Works

01

Existing Cameras & SOPs Are Integrated

We integrate your current CCTV infrastructure and operational protocols into the Sky Sentinel platform.

02

24x7 Surveillance Begins

Our surveillance team continuously monitors designated zones in real time.

03

AI & Operators Detect Incidents

Security breaches, suspicious activity, SOP failures, utility issues, and operational violations are detected automatically.
